Polynyms are related ideas.

Some occur naturally…
…others are artefacts of years of study.
We collect them all with fun puzzles & games.
Why?
We seek deeper meaning in language and between ourselves. By mapping idea sets across cultures and disciplines, we can find the hidden connections that bring us closer together.
Is this AI?
No. We build knowledge bases for AI systems to use. The web teaches poorly, like the infamous racist AI by IBM. We offer a manicured knowledge garden as an alternative to a web wasteland. In the spirit of MIT's Open Mind project, we feel building knowledge for AI can be open, fun and engaging.
Thanks for playing and contributing to humanity!
